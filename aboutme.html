About me

I care about the quiet details that make digital products feel trustworthy — hierarchy, pacing, and language that gets out of the way.

A bit of background

I’m Sophia, a builder focused on web experiences that balance clarity with atmosphere. I enjoy taking a rough idea from sketch to polished page — structure first, then motion, type, and tone.

This site is my home base for selected work, experiments, and ways to reach me. I’m always refining the craft: cleaner layouts, stronger storytelling, and interfaces people can navigate without friction.

What I focus on

  • Interface design Layout, typography, and visual rhythm for pages that feel composed instead of crowded.
  • Front-end build Semantic HTML, accessible patterns, and CSS that holds up across screen sizes.
  • Product storytelling Turning features and process into narratives that are easy to scan and remember.
